# Final project proposal

- Due Friday by 12pm
 - Points 100
 - Submitting a text entry box or a file upload
 - Available Jul 16 at 9pm - Jul 24 at 12pm

**CS 5800 Final Project**

We will end this course with a Final Project, where you will put together the algorithms, techniques, skills, and insights you have learned in this course to answer a question that is important to you.

This is a group Project, you will work in your groups of 2. 

For your Final Project, you will find **an application** and **pose a question** that is of interest to you related to this application, and answer it to the best of your ability by applying one or more topics that you can find in our Algorithms book. _For example, I am interested in soccer tournaments and I would like to know how we can schedule the various matches between teams so that we minimize the traveling time of players._

The Final Project is incredibly open-ended, and all I ask for (and insist upon) is that the question you choose is important to each of you.

**Select your project with your group-mates****,** submit your proposal and your plans to complete this project. You need to submit a **Final Project Proposal** (roughly 1000 words) which must contain each of the following:

- A **context** that will help the reader understand your topic, and why this topic is personally relevant to you.  Provide one paragraph for each member of your team. (Do not just mention that you like this application, give us more information about this application and the motivation behind your project.)
- A clearly defined **question** that you wish to answer (what is the problem that you would like to solve or optimize).
- A clearly defined **scope** for your project: what do you intend to examine and what do you intend not to examine?
- A clear **description** of what you plan to do over the next few weeks till the last week of the semester, with specific details.
- How the project will be divided among the various members of the group, what would be the task of each student.
- Explain how your project relates to the topics found in your Algorithms book.

**By Friday 08/14 you will submit your code and presentation to Canvas (one submission per group). In addition to the group submission, each student will record a video individually explaining their contribution to the project and explaining how they solved their part**. The last two week of the semester will be reserved for your **Final Project Presentations**.  Each group will have 15 minutes to share their work, in addition to 5 minutes for questions.

Your Final Project will be marked out of **50**, and will be worth 12% of your final course grade. 

Your Final Project Presentation will be evaluated on how you solved your question/problem and how well you explained your methodology and your results during your presentation including:

- effective use of visual aids (e.g. PowerPoint Slides)
- slides organization
- your methodology (Do NOT show your code on a slide and do not explain your code)
- Effective use of an example
- keeping to the required time limit
- your confidence and understanding of what you’re presenting
- clear communication (Reading your words from a prewritten paper/card/phone/… will make you lose the attention of your audience; and will lower your grade.)

Your presentation should include:

- **Introduction:** a context that will help us understand your chosen topic, with a clearly-defined question inspired by your issue with a rationale that explains what you are doing and why.
- **Analysis:** a description of the methods you used to **gather your data** and/or **solve your problem**. What did you do and why.  Make sure you submit the relevant computer program and a pseudocode of this program. Mention how you tested your program and list the inputs and outputs of this program.
- **Conclusion:** based on your analysis, answer your question. Then discuss the weaknesses and limitations of your project, and suggest avenues for future research. 

_Please note:_

- _Divide the work on this project equally among group members._
- _Saying all group members worked on everything does not constitute good teamwork_
- _The grade of a student depends on_
    - _their contribution to the project_
    - _their performance during the presentation_
    - _their engagement on presentation day by asking questions to the presenting teams_
    - _their contribution in evaluating presentations and students by filling the google form for each team._
